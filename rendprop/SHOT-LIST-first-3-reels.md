# Shot List — First 3 Reels (film-yourself → swap)

Codex's three concepts, restructured for the swap pipeline: open on the product, one continuous host take ≤ 20 s, demo as a cutaway, keyword CTA. Spoken lines are what **you** say on camera; they come out in her voice.

Film two takes of each. Say "Reel one, take one" before you start (trimmed later). Check for mirrors.

---

## Reel 1 — "Your $400 photographer hates this" — MIA · TOUR

**Location:** a real listing or any bright, empty-ish house. Stand near a doorway with a room behind you.

**0:00–0:02 Hook b-roll (not you):** a Rendprop fly-through of the same house if you have it, otherwise the best published tour.
On-screen: **Your $400 photographer hates this.**

**0:02–0:20 Host take — you, fixed camera, chest-up, Mia energy (flat, dry, half-beat after the joke):**

> "I filmed this whole house on my phone. Took four minutes. [beat]
> Rendprop turned the walkthrough into a tour that scrolls like a movie — tap a room, jump straight to it, send one link.
> No photographer. No scheduling. No Tuesday.
> Comment TOUR and I'll send you the app."

**Demo cutaway (edit, 0:08–0:14):** screen recording of the real hosted tour — a scroll, one tap into a tagged room.
On-screen: **Walkthrough → tour → one link**

**0:18–0:20 CTA frame:** **Comment TOUR** · "Mia · Rendprop AI host"

**Caption:** Four minutes on a phone. One link to send. Mia is an AI-generated host; the tour is a real Rendprop tour. Comment TOUR for the app. #Rendprop #ListingMarketing #RealEstateMarketing

## Reel 2 — "The empty room problem" — AVA · STAGE

**Location:** a genuinely empty room with good window light. You stand off to one side so the room is visible.

**0:00–0:02 Hook b-roll (not you):** the staged result of *this* room, if you've run it through AI Photo Studio. Otherwise a real before/after pair from the app.
On-screen: **Great floor. Now what?**

**0:02–0:20 Host take — you, fixed camera, waist-up, Ava energy (warm, quick, a smile on the punchline). Open palm toward the room, one glance down at the floor on "floor":**

> "This room has potential. Right now the floor's doing all the work. [smile]
> Rendprop's photo studio drops furniture in so buyers can picture living here — and it keeps the original right next to it, labeled, so nobody's getting fooled.
> Comment STAGE and I'll send you the app."

**Demo cutaway (edit, 0:09–0:15):** original photo and staged photo side by side, "Original" / "AI virtually staged" labels visible.
On-screen: **Help buyers picture the space**

**0:18–0:20 CTA frame:** **Comment STAGE** · "Ava · Rendprop AI host"

**Caption:** Empty rooms don't sell themselves. Staged view is AI, labeled, with the original beside it. Ava is an AI-generated host. Comment STAGE for the app. #Rendprop #VirtualStaging #ListingPhotos

## Reel 3 — "I filmed this restaurant in three minutes" — AVA · SPACE

**Location:** a local restaurant, café or gym, off-hours, with the owner's OK (and offer them the tour for free — that's a user). Walk-and-talk, slow, two hands on the phone, toward the best light.

**0:00–0:02 Hook b-roll (not you):** the fly-through of the space.
On-screen: **I filmed this restaurant in 3 minutes.**

**0:02–0:20 Host take — slow walk-and-talk, full body to waist-up, Ava energy:**

> "Every restaurant has photos. Almost none have this. [gesture at the room]
> Walk through once with your phone, and Rendprop turns it into a tour people can actually move through — for the website, the Google listing, the booking page.
> Same thing works for gyms, venues, shops.
> Comment SPACE and I'll send it to you."

**Demo cutaway (edit, 0:10–0:15):** the hosted tour of the space, a scroll and a room jump.
On-screen: **Restaurants · gyms · venues · shops**

**0:18–0:20 CTA frame:** **Comment SPACE** · "Ava · Rendprop AI host"

**Caption:** Photos show the space. A tour lets people walk it. Ava is an AI-generated host; the tour is real. Comment SPACE for the app. #Rendprop #RestaurantMarketing #SmallBusiness

## Before you shoot — checklist

- [ ] 9:16, 1080p, 30 fps. Tripod or a steady prop for Reels 1 and 2.
- [ ] Fitted mid-tone shirt, no logos, no hat, no glasses.
- [ ] Window light on your face.
- [ ] Scan the frame for mirrors, glass, dark screens.
- [ ] Quiet room, AC off.
- [ ] Say the reel/take number first. Two takes each.
- [ ] Separately: screen-record the real tour / staging / app flow for each cutaway, and export the fly-through b-roll.
- [ ] Keep every take under 25 seconds.
